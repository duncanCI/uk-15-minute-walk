        Read this before quoting a number

        The places come from OpenStreetMap. Where mapping is thin, a street can look cut off when it is not. When you pick a council, the line under the headline shows how many of each place the data holds there. Check any low figure against NHS or council lists.

        • A food shop is a supermarket or convenience store. GP or pharmacy also counts clinics and hospitals. A gym includes fitness and sports centres. Schools include school buildings grouped into sites.
        • Walks follow footways, paths and streets at 4.8 km/h, so 15 minutes is 1,200 m. A street counts if its midpoint is within the walk.
        • Shares are by residential street length, not by people. A street of tenements counts the same as a cul-de-sac.
        • Zoomed out, each dot is a hexagon of about 0.7 km² coloured by its typical street. Zoom in to see every street.
        • Places up to 2 km outside a council count, so streets near a boundary can use a shop over the line.
