    Read this before quoting a number

    The places come from OpenStreetMap. Where mapping is thin, a street can look cut off when it is not. Inverclyde, for example, has 7 GP, pharmacy, clinic or hospital locations in the data. Check any low figure against NHS or council lists before you use it.

    • A food shop is a supermarket or convenience store. GP or pharmacy also counts clinics and hospitals. A gym includes fitness and sports centres.
    • Distances follow footways, paths and streets. Walk speed is 4.8 km/h, so 15 minutes is 1,200 m.
    • A street counts if its midpoint is within the walk. Shares are by street length, not by people, so a street of tenements counts the same as a cul-de-sac.
    • Schools are school buildings grouped into sites, because school grounds were not in the source data.
    • Places up to 2 km outside the region count, so streets near a boundary can use a shop over the line.
